Monitor arm installation checklist: before the first lift

The safest installation checklist is specific about what must be known and honest about what a checklist cannot inspect.

Published October 27, 2026Document-based editorial · No hands-on testing

Before opening the hardware

  1. Write the exact arm model and clamp or grommet version.
  2. Read the complete manufacturer manual and identify the intended attachment route.
  3. Confirm the desk material and whether the desk maker permits the proposed concentrated load.
  4. Record the monitor load without its stand, attached accessories, VESA pattern and screen depth.
  5. Clear a support surface where the monitor can rest while the plate is attached.

Do not begin by removing the monitor stand and hoping the mount will solve the remaining questions. The installation path should already be identified, including the correct screw and spacer route. A universal torque number is not supplied by this site because hardware, thread, material and manufacturer instructions differ.

Desk and base preflight

  • Top contact is flat and clean.
  • Underside contact is visible and free of rails, aprons, drawers or cable trays.
  • Clamp thickness or grommet dimensions are within the exact manual’s configuration.
  • The arm base will not collide with the wall, shelf or desk accessory.
  • The desk does not flex, crack, chip or show damage at the intended load area.

The VIVO product page lists a 3.25-inch maximum desk thickness but does not establish every minimum or material condition. The Ergotron LX manual illustrates separate clamp positions and should be read for the actual thickness. A result from the site’s preliminary checker can flag an obvious mismatch; it cannot see the underside or judge desk strength.

Monitor plate preflight

  • Support the screen while removing its stand.
  • Confirm the exact VESA holes and any approved spacers.
  • Compare screw diameter, pitch, length and head with the monitor and arm documents.
  • Seat the plate flat without covering connectors or forcing a recess.
  • Tighten by the documented method; stop if a screw binds or bottoms out.

A screw that starts is not necessarily a correct screw. Do not use a power driver to overcome an uncertain fit. If the screen has a deep recess, an offset pattern or an adapter, identify the exact approved configuration before assembly. The plate should not twist when the screen is supported.

First movement check

  1. With the screen supported, make the smallest intended movement and observe the base, joints and plate.
  2. Set the arm tension according to the exact manual and the complete mounted load.
  3. Route cables with enough slack for the intended movement and no pinch at joints.
  4. Check the highest, lowest, nearest and farthest useful positions.
  5. Look for wall, shelf, desk-edge, lamp and neighboring-screen collisions.

Move slowly. A monitor arm is a loaded mechanism, and the installation may reveal a problem only when the screen moves away from the base. If a clamp shifts, the desk deforms, the screen drops or the VESA plate separates, stop and support the load. Do not treat a repeated adjustment as a substitute for diagnosing the attachment.

After the first day and after changes

Inspect the same points after the first day of use and after changing the monitor, adding an accessory, moving the base or raising a sit-stand desk. Look for movement at the clamp, new marks or cracks in the desktop, loose hardware, cable strain and a change in the screen’s level. Record the result so a slow change is visible rather than becoming normal.

There is no universal tightening or torque setting for every monitor arm installation. Use the manufacturer’s documented hardware and adjustment procedure for the exact model.

A compact sign-off

  1. Model and manual revision recorded: yes / no
  2. Load and VESA source recorded: yes / no
  3. Desk material and contact geometry confirmed: yes / no
  4. Cables and collision path checked: yes / no
  5. Initial movement stable and unchanged after inspection: yes / no
  6. Unresolved question sent to manufacturer: __________________
