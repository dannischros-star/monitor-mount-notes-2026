How to measure a desk before buying a monitor arm

Measure the place where the arm will live, not the part of the desk that is easiest to reach with a tape measure.

Published October 27, 2026Document-based editorial · No hands-on testing

Make two sketches

Use one top view and one side view. In the top view, draw the desk outline, wall, window trim, shelf, existing monitor, laptop, lamp and the location where the arm base might sit. In the side view, draw the desktop thickness, edge shape, underside apron or rail, wall gap and the screen’s intended position. Leave blank space around the drawing for measurements.

A sketch is useful because the arm moves in more than one direction. A single depth number cannot show whether an elbow will collide with a wall or whether a screen will cross the desk edge. Write the date and the location of each measurement; do not combine a front-edge number with a rear-edge number as though they describe one surface.

Worksheet: desk and edge

  • Desk width at the proposed base location: ______ in / ______ mm
  • Usable depth from front edge to wall or obstruction: ______ in / ______ mm
  • Desktop thickness at the clamp contact: ______ in / ______ mm
  • Flat top contact length: ______ in / ______ mm
  • Flat underside contact length: ______ in / ______ mm
  • Edge shape: square / rounded / beveled / framed / unknown
  • Underside obstruction and distance from edge: __________________________
  • Desk material and manufacturer source: _________________________________

Measure with the desk empty enough to see the contact area. Use a straight edge or a careful visual check for a bevel. If the underside is covered by a drawer, remove or open it only as the desk instructions permit. A measurement you cannot see or repeat is worth less than a smaller measurement you can explain.

Worksheet: user and screen

  • Seated eye height from floor: ______ in / ______ mm
  • Standing eye height from floor: ______ in / ______ mm
  • Monitor weight without stand: ______ lb
  • Attached accessories included in load: _________________________________
  • Monitor depth: ______ in / ______ mm
  • VESA pattern: 75 × 75 / 100 × 100 / other / unknown
  • Screen width and height: ______ × ______
  • Desired screen center, seated: __________________________
  • Desired screen center, standing: ________________________

Use the monitor’s manual for weight and VESA information before using a scale or tape. Record the source, because a boxed weight or a number that includes the original stand can mislead the mount selection. Screen dimensions help draw the clearance envelope; they do not replace the VESA and load checks.

Worksheet: clearance and movement

Mark the arm base, the screen’s closest position to the wall, the farthest intended reach and the elbow’s route. A manufacturer’s maximum extension is a boundary, not a required position. Check where the display sits when you rotate it, tilt it, place it in portrait orientation or move the desk between sitting and standing.

  • Wall or shelf clearance at the arm base: ______ in
  • Screen-to-wall distance at intended position: ______ in
  • Cable path and service slack: __________________________
  • Collision with lamp, shelf, laptop or neighbor: yes / no / unknown
  • Sit-stand travel checked: yes / no / not applicable
  • Second monitor or accessory load: _______________________

Turn measurements into questions

The worksheet does not decide the installation on its own. Compare the desk thickness and contact geometry with the exact clamp or grommet manual. Compare the loaded monitor with both ends of the arm’s range. Compare the screen depth and desired positions with the manufacturer drawing. If the desk material, underside, VESA pattern or screw details remain unknown, the next action is documentation or a manufacturer question, not a purchase.

A precise measurement can still describe an unsuitable surface. Measurements improve the question; they do not certify the answer.

Save the worksheet with the setup

Keep the worksheet with the monitor model, arm model, manual revision and any manufacturer response. Revisit it when you change the screen, add an accessory, move the clamp or convert the desk to sit-stand use. The same desk can become a different mounting configuration when the load or position changes.
