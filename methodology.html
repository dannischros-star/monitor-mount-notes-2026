Editorial methodology

Show the source.
Keep the caveat.

A specification is useful only when the model, meaning and limits stay attached to it.

Published October 27, 2026

1. Start with the exact model

We identify the SKU and mounting arrangement before comparing specifications. A family name can contain single, dual, tall-pole and alternate-clamp versions. A number from one version does not silently transfer to another.

2. Prefer manufacturer documentation

Our first comparison uses manufacturer product information, assembly manuals and dimensional drawings. Source links sit beside the discussion or in its source list. When the Ergotron product page returned HTTP 403 during our check, we used accessible manufacturer PDFs and disclosed that limitation.

“Specs checked Oct 27, 2026” means we consulted the listed materials on that date. It does not imply a manufacturer update, current inventory, independent lab verification or continuing live monitoring. Older document revision dates are retained where they help identify the source.

3. Separate fact from interpretation

Load limits and dimensions are attributed specifications. A suggestion about which design may suit a routine is editorial interpretation. We have not conducted hands-on tests, measured stability, or assessed long-term reliability. We publish no test scores, awards, star ratings or “best overall” claims.

4. Preserve unknowns

An absent minimum is left unknown. A screen-size headline does not override a weight limit. Inconsistent clamp intervals prompt review. We conservatively omit details we cannot support, and our checker reports numerical mismatches separately from unresolved installation questions.

5. Keep the tool narrow

The checker uses three reader inputs and fixed, dated specifications. It does not inspect the desk, test material strength, approve fasteners, assess screen depth or certify safety. An in-range result still requires the exact manuals and any necessary manufacturer confirmation.

6. Correct the record

Readers can submit a public correction through GitHub Issues. Changes to published files appear in the repository’s commit history. We do not promise a fixed update schedule; check the current manufacturer instructions before acting.

Commercial separation

No paid links, sponsorships or affiliate commissions are currently implemented. If a future commercial relationship is approved and used, we will identify the affected content and update the disclosures. A relationship will not be described as a product test.
