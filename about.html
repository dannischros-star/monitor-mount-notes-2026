About Monitor Mount Notes

A useful corner
of the internet.

A new independent editorial website about monitor arms and the small desks they attach to.

Published October 27, 2026

Monitor Mount Notes is for readers planning a compact workspace who want to understand fit before buying hardware. The first edition focuses on one-monitor desks, a practical selection guide, and a documented comparison of two specific arm models.

Our contribution is the explanation between the numbers: why a minimum load matters, why a desktop needs more than a thickness check, and why pole height and lift range are different measurements. We link manufacturer documents so you can trace those claims.

What is here today

The site launched on October 27, 2026. It has no established audience or traffic history to report. We have not physically tested the featured products, measured their performance or received product samples. There are no current paid links or sponsored placements.

The illustrations and prose are original to this website. Diagrams are explanatory schematics, not product photographs or engineered installation drawings. Product names identify the subjects of discussion; they do not imply endorsement or a relationship with their manufacturers.

A small, inspectable website

This is a static site with no account system, advertising scripts or analytics. The desk-fit checker runs in your browser. Read our editorial methodology, disclosures and privacy notice for the boundaries.

Corrections and feedback

Use the public GitHub Issues page to report a broken link, an unclear explanation or a specification that has changed. Include the model and a manufacturer source where possible. Issues are public: do not post personal details, account credentials or private purchase records. A GitHub account may be required to post.
